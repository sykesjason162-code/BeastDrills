Basics
Trains against
Which of your Receiver tallies this drill is a fix for{% if scrimmage_available %}. The scrimmage recommender ranks what keeps happening to you and pulls drills that claim it{% endif %} -- e.g. a throw-tech drill trains against got_thrown. Optional.
Training for
Which of your Giver tallies this drill builds. Trains against says which mistake it stops; this says which skill it grows -- e.g. a hit-confirm drill trains for hit_confirm. Optional, and a drill can reasonably have both, one, or neither.

Parameter Settings
Health and meter at the start of every rep. Mine is you, theirs is the dummy. Blank means the game's own default — full health, full Drive, no Super — and that default is applied every session, so the last drill's burnout setup cannot follow you into this one.
Recovery is how the gauge behaves afterwards, and it defaults to refill: the value comes back every rep, which is what makes a drill a drill. Standard is match behaviour — rep two starts wherever rep one left you — and fixed locks the gauge where it is.
